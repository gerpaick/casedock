Privacy · Draft

What casedock stores, and what stays yours.

This is the short, honest version. A full EU-legal review lands before public launch — until then, treat this page as a draft that describes how the product behaves today.

Account

What we store

  • Your email address — used only for login and account recovery.
  • Everything you create: Inbox items, Cases, decisions, execution items, notes, focus selections.

Passwords are hashed. We cannot read them.

Hosting

Where it lives

casedock runs on a single VPS. Data is stored in PostgreSQL on the same host. Hosting region and backup policy will be confirmed here before public launch.

Tracking

Cookies and third parties

  • One session cookie — required for login. No tracking cookies.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers during beta.
  • We do not sell, share, or send your data to third parties.

Your control

Delete or export

Your data is yours. To export or delete everything, email privacy@casedock.local from the address on your account. Self-serve export and deletion in Settings is planned before public launch.

Beta

Status

Last updated: 2026-07-30. casedock is in private beta — this policy may change before public launch, and changes will be announced in-product.
