How casedock works.

The short version: capture everything into the Inbox, turn what matters into a Case, then work on one Case at a time. This page is a basic starter — fuller guides land closer to public launch.

Start here

Capture

Press c anywhere in the app (or open Inbox) and type whatever just landed — a title is enough. No required fields, no due dates, no priority flags.

Triage

Turn an Inbox item into a Case

Open an Inbox item and choose what to do with it. Convert to Case moves it into the workspace with space for spec, decisions, execution, and private notes.

Focus

Pick one main Case for today

On Focus choose one main Case and up to two supporting tracks. The Board will surface that main Case first so you don't have to decide again where to look.

Re-entry

Find anything

Use Search to find any Case by title or content. Each Case keeps its spec, decisions, execution, and private notes together so you can pick it back up without reconstructing context.

Contact

Still stuck?

Email help@casedock.local — a real person reads every message. This address will switch to the production domain before public launch.
